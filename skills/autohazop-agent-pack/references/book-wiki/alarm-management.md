## Alarm lifecycle

Use alarm philosophy, identification, rationalization, design, implementation, operation, maintenance, monitoring, assessment, and MOC as the control loop.

## Operator response

Do not treat an alarm as a strong safeguard unless the alarm is rationalized, clear, timely, prioritized, independent enough, trained, tested, and response time is validated.

## Performance review

Track nuisance alarms, standing alarms, alarm floods, shelved/suppressed alarms, stale setpoints, alarm loading, and weak consequence/action wording.
