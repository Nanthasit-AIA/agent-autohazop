## Inherent safety prompts

Ask first whether inventory, energy, hazardous material, conditions, congestion, complexity, or human reliance can be eliminated, minimized, substituted, moderated, or simplified.

## Siting/layout prompts

Review occupied buildings, separation, congestion, drainage, escalation, access/egress, emergency response, and vulnerability to fire/explosion/toxic effects.

## HAZOP/LOPA use

Prefer risk reduction at the source before adding alarms, procedures, or SIS; document rejected inherently safer options when add-on layers are chosen.

## Answer Pattern

When using this wiki, answer with:

- `Decision`: the best current engineering decision or review status.
- `Basis`: source slug(s), page count/source note, and assumptions.
- `Missing data`: project criteria, tags, drawings, calculations, inspection/proof-test evidence, or data tables needed.
- `Cautions`: dependency, uncertainty, applicability, or OCR/manual-lookup limitations.
- `Next action`: specific review, calculation, field check, or documentation update.
