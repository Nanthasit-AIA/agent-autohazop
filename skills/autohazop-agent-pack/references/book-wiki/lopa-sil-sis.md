## Scenario basis

Normalize each LOPA scenario to one initiating event, one consequence, enabling conditions, conditional modifiers, and candidate layers.

## IPL basis

Credit a layer only when it is effective, independent, auditable, and supported by project-approved PFD/credit evidence.

## SIS basis

Treat SIF/SIS claims as lifecycle evidence questions: SIF definition, safe state, target SIL/PFD, proof testing, validation, bypass/MOC, and independence from BPCS.

- `Decision`: the best current engineering decision or review status.
