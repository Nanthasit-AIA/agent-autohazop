## Human error stance

Treat human error as a symptom to investigate; prefer changing situations, interfaces, designs, procedures, training systems, workload, and supervision over blaming people.

## Incident investigation

Collect evidence, establish timeline, separate immediate/underlying/root causes, identify barrier failures, and write specific corrective actions with owners and verification.

## Learning loop

Check whether recommendations prevent recurrence, update procedures/training/design, feed HAZOP prompts, and preserve organizational memory.

## Answer Pattern

When using this wiki, answer with:

- `Decision`: the best current engineering decision or review status.
- `Basis`: source slug(s), page count/source note, and assumptions.
- `Missing data`: project criteria, tags, drawings, calculations, inspection/proof-test evidence, or data tables needed.
- `Cautions`: dependency, uncertainty, applicability, or OCR/manual-lookup limitations.
- `Next action`: specific review, calculation, field check, or documentation update.
