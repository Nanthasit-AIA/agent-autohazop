## Risk criteria

Separate individual risk, societal risk, scenario risk, corporate criteria, regulatory criteria, ALARP/ALAP arguments, and cost-benefit logic.

## QRA escalation

Escalate when scenario coupling, cumulative risk, facility siting, toxic/flammable consequences, or high-stakes mitigation decisions exceed LOPA confidence.

## Model discipline

Require clear source terms, event trees, fault trees, consequence models, frequency basis, uncertainty, and sensitivity checks.

## Answer Pattern

When using this wiki, answer with:

- `Decision`: the best current engineering decision or review status.
- `Basis`: source slug(s), page count/source note, and assumptions.
- `Missing data`: project criteria, tags, drawings, calculations, inspection/proof-test evidence, or data tables needed.
- `Cautions`: dependency, uncertainty, applicability, or OCR/manual-lookup limitations.
- `Next action`: specific review, calculation, field check, or documentation update.
