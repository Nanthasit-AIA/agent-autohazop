It is not a substitute for:

- ISA-5.1 symbol interpretation.
- ISA-5.4 loop-diagram compliance.
- IEC 61511 SIF/SIS lifecycle review.
- Company risk matrix, IEL, or IPL criteria.
- Project P&IDs, loop diagrams, cause-and-effect, control narrative, SRS, or commissioning records.
