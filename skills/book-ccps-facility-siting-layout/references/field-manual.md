## How To Attack A HAZOP Row

## Sharp Questions

- Can the hazard be eliminated, substituted, minimized, moderated, simplified, segregated, or relocated before adding protection?
- What inventory, pressure, temperature, material, layout, congestion, drainage, or occupied-building exposure drives the risk?
- Is the safeguard compensating for a design/layout choice that should be challenged?
- Does the recommendation reduce the hazard at source or merely add an administrative layer?

## Anti-Patterns To Kill

- Adding alarms/procedures while leaving avoidable inventory, energy, or exposure unchanged.
- Treating facility siting/layout as fixed without stating project constraints.
- Ignoring escalation paths, drainage, congestion, access, and occupied-building exposure.

## Row Moves

- Insert an inherent-safety option screen before recommending add-on safeguards.
- Separate source reduction, exposure reduction, escalation control, and emergency response.
- Request layout/siting basis when consequence path depends on occupancy or separation.

## Hard Decision Gates

- Whether hazard can be eliminated, substituted, minimized, moderated, simplified, segregated, or relocated.
- Whether occupied building, congestion, drainage, escalation, access/egress, or emergency response exposure is relevant.
- Whether a safeguard is compensating for a design/layout issue that should be challenged earlier.
