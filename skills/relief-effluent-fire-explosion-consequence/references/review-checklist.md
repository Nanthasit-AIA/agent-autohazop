# Review Checklist - Relief, Effluent, Fire and Consequence Review

- [ ] For relief scenarios, identify protected equipment, credible causes, relief path, disposal/effluent destination, capacity basis, inspection/testing, and blocked/fouled/two-phase flags.
- [ ] For fire/explosion/consequence, classify mechanism and route to source term, dispersion, toxic, flammable, thermal radiation, overpressure, VCE, flash fire, or BLEVE analysis.
- [ ] Do not credit relief or fire protection without design, inspection, maintenance, and scenario-specific effectiveness evidence.

## Final QA

- [ ] Project-specific data were used where required.
- [ ] Missing basis is explicit.
- [ ] Dependencies/common-cause issues are visible.
- [ ] Recommendation is action-oriented and auditable.
- [ ] Source slugs are cited.
